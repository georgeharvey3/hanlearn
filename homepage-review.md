# Homepage Review

## Visual Design Issues

1. **Color scheme feels dated** — The dominant dark red/maroon background with tan/cream cards gives a "Chinese restaurant menu" aesthetic rather than a modern learning platform. It feels stereotypical rather than professional.

2. **Typography is weak** — Body text uses what appears to be a serif or default font with inconsistent sizing. Headings are small and don't establish clear hierarchy. The colored text (red/green headings) reduces readability.

3. **Hero banner is cluttered** — The calligraphy background image, while thematically relevant, makes the overlaid text hard to read. The semi-transparent dark overlay isn't dark enough for good contrast. The heading itself is too wordy.

4. **No visual polish** — No shadows, no rounded corners, no modern spacing. Cards/sections have thin borders that feel like HTML tables. The overall layout reads as "Bootstrap 3 era."

5. **Screenshots are tiny and blurry** — The product screenshots in the feature sections are too small to actually show what the app does. They look like low-res captures.

6. **Footer feels abandoned** — "© George Harvey 2020" with a plain Gmail contact address undermines credibility.

## Layout & Content Issues

7. **No clear value proposition above the fold** — The hero text is generic ("A vocabulary learning application"). It doesn't say *why* this is better than Anki, Pleco, or Duolingo.

8. **"Join HanLearn!" section is weak** — "Create a free account to edit translations" isn't compelling. Also has a typo: "repitition" should be "repetition."

9. **Two CTAs compete** — "Try it out" and "Sign Up" sit next to each other with no visual distinction or explanation of the difference.

10. **The Chengyu quiz is interactive but unexplained** — It's dropped on the page with no context about what a chengyu is for non-Chinese-speakers.

11. **Feature sections alternate left-right** — A common pattern, but with low-quality screenshots and no icons or illustrations, it feels flat and repetitive.

## What Should Change

- **New color palette**: Move to a clean, modern palette — white/light gray backgrounds with a single accent color (maybe a refined red or teal). Drop the maroon background.
- **Modern typography**: Use a system font stack or a clean sans-serif (Inter, Noto Sans). Increase font sizes and spacing.
- **Redesign the hero**: Full-width with a clean illustration or subtle pattern, a punchy 5-7 word headline, a one-line subtitle, and a single prominent CTA.
- **Replace screenshots with illustrations or high-quality mockups** showing the app in device frames.
- **Add social proof**: User count, testimonials, or at minimum, a GitHub stars badge.
- **Fix the footer**: Update the year, add proper links, make the contact professional.
- **Fix typos**: "repitition" → "repetition", "pronunctiation" → "pronunciation".
- **Simplify the page**: Cut the word count significantly. Let visuals do more work.
